Ethanol-Water Separation and Recycle Material Balance Model



Developed an Excel-based steady-state material balance model for an ethanol-water separation process including recycle and purge streams. Evaluated the effects of recycle fraction and ethanol recovery against the product ethanol concentration.



Model



* 100 kg/hr fresh feed
* 30 wt% ethanol
* 90% baseline ethanol recovery
* 5% water recovery to product
* 20% baseline recycle fraction
* Steady-state operation
* No chemical reaction



Excel includes



* Overall and component material balances
* Single-pass recycle calculations
* Steady-state recycle and purge calculations
* Excel iterative calculation for recycle convergence
* Separator and overall process mass-balance validation
* Sensitivity analysis of recycle fraction and ethanol recovery
* Process performance visualization using Excel charts
